Aria — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-16 · applies to Aria for iOS

Aria is a music player for your own files and your own music server. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The developer does not receive any data from the app.

What stays on your device

Your imported music, playlists, favorites, listening history, EQ profiles and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are included in your device backups according to your iOS backup settings, and nowhere else.

What is sent to a server you configure

Aria can optionally connect to a music server that you run or choose — for example Navidrome, Airsonic, Gonic, or Aria's open-source companion backend. Nothing is sent anywhere until you enter a server address yourself. Once you do, the app sends to that server only:

  • your search text, so the server can search your library;
  • for "Ask Your Library", metadata about your local library (titles, artists, albums, play counts) so the server can index it;
  • your username and a salted authentication token for Subsonic servers. Your password itself is never transmitted. It is stored in the iOS Keychain.

What that server does with the data is governed by whoever operates it — usually you.

Purchases

Aria Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. The app learns only whether the purchase is active. The developer never sees your payment details or Apple ID.

Tracking

Aria does not track you, does not use identifiers for advertising, and does not share data with anyone. There is nothing to opt out of.

Children

Aria collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Aria is open source; the code that governs what the app sends is public at github.com/evince55/Aria.
